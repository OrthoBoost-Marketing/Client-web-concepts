What we do differently at Chang Orthodontics

The plan is measured, not estimated

A digital scan replaces the impression tray. That scan drives the treatment plan, the appliance, and the timeline, so what you are shown at the consultation is what the case is built on.

See the technology we use

Airway is assessed, not assumed

Dr. Chang holds a master's-level education in airway orthodontics and has published peer-reviewed research measuring 3-D airway change before and after treatment. Growing patients are evaluated for it as a matter of course.

Read about airway and expansion

One practice, one orthodontist, every visit

This is a single office with a single orthodontist. Dr. Chang plans your case and finishes it, and you are not passed between providers. After-school appointments are available, and someone answers the phone through lunch.
