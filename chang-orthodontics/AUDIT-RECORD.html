What to expect when Dr. Chang compares them

The brand holds across all three: the same navy, the same logo, the same verified facts, the same eleven approved photographs. What changes is the arrangement — section order, hero pattern, density, type scale, and how the photography is cropped and paced.

So the three will read as arrangements of one practice rather than three different-looking websites. That is the intent. Choosing a direction means choosing a layout and a rhythm, not a different brand. Concept B is the published direction; A and C stand as the alternatives.

Drop the noindex

Deliberate on all three while they are unlisted previews. Removing it belongs to the launch sequence, alongside compiling the CDN libraries into the bundle, which the output spec requires.

Client

Review quotes

A hundred five-star reviews sit on the profile and not one is transcribed. Every testimonial slot sitewide ships as a visible placeholder until they are pulled. The largest single gap between concept and launchable.

Client

UCSF mark sign-off

The file is public domain, but universities restrict third-party use and the mark can read as endorsement. The UCSF Medical Center wordmark was already rejected as a different legal entity. The ABO mark is not obtainable at all, so that slot stays typographic.

Client

Social profile URLs

No concept links any. The footer is the only approved home for them, and the rule is to link only profiles that are actually maintained. If none exist, all three footers are already correct.

Client

Service-card imagery

Four of the six cards do not show the service: a community booth, an empty consult room, an office interior. The eleven approved photographs contain no braces or aligner frames, so only stock can satisfy the rule — permitted here and nowhere else.

Client

Confirm the hero photograph

All three heroes use the same frame. It does show Dr. Chang with a child in the office, but it is a jersey handoff rather than treatment. Worth one look before launch, since the standard asks for the service being delivered.
