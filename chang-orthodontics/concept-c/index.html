Meet Dr. Michael Chang

Dr. Chang trained at UCSF and has been treating patients for more than fifteen years. He is board certified by the American Board of Orthodontics, and since 2018 he has taught the next generation of orthodontists as a Clinical Professor at UCSF.

His published, peer-reviewed research looks at how the airway changes before and after orthodontic treatment, which is why an airway assessment is part of how he plans a case here.
