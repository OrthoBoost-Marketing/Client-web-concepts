Meet Dr. Michael Chang, DDS

Fifteen years in practice, and still teaching the next class.

Dr. Chang completed the seven-year combined dental and orthodontic program at UCSF, and he has been back on its faculty as a clinical professor since 2018. His published research looks at how orthodontic treatment changes the airway, which is why the airway is part of the conversation here and not an afterthought.

He lives in San Carlos with his family. If you catch him outside the office, he is usually in Giants or 49ers gear.

  • Seven-year combined dental and orthodontic program, UCSF
  • Platinum Plus Invisalign Provider
  • Published, peer-reviewed research on airway change during treatment

More about Dr. Chang

One orthodontist, every appointment.

There are no rotating associates here. Dr. Chang plans your treatment, makes the adjustments, and is the person you speak to when something needs changing. That continuity is the whole reason this practice stayed small.

Why families choose Chang Orthodontics

Appointments that fit around a school day.

Most of our patients are still in school, so after-school appointments are part of how the schedule is built rather than a favor you have to ask for. Wait times are kept short on purpose, because a checked-in patient sitting in a waiting room is a scheduling failure.

Treatments

Orthodontics for children, teens, and adults.

Braces

Metal and ceramic braces for every age, planned from a 3D scan rather than a putty impression. Still the most predictable way to correct a complex bite.

Learn more

Invisalign

Dr. Chang is a Platinum Plus Invisalign Provider, a tier based on how many cases a doctor actually completes. Aligners for adults, teens, and children.

Learn more

Early Orthodontics

Phase 1 treatment, expanders, and space maintainers for children whose jaws are still growing. Catching a problem early often means a shorter second phase.

Learn more

Kids & Teens

Most of our patients are school age. Appointments are scheduled around the school day, and parents get a straight answer about timing before anything starts.

Learn more

Adult Orthodontics

Discreet aligners and ceramic braces, complex bite correction, and retreatment for adults whose teeth shifted after earlier work. Nobody is too old for this.

Learn more

Airway & Expansion

Palatal expansion, including MARPE and MSE, planned with the airway in view. This is the subject of Dr. Chang's published research, not a service bolted on.
