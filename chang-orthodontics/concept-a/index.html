The orthodontist

Fifteen years in practice, and still teaching the next class.

Dr. Chang completed the seven-year combined dental and orthodontic program at UCSF, and he has been back on its faculty as a clinical professor since 2018. His published research looks at how orthodontic treatment changes the airway, which is why the airway is part of the conversation here and not an afterthought.

He lives in San Carlos with his family. If you catch him outside the office, he is usually in Giants or 49ers gear.

More About Dr. Chang

Credentials

  • Certified Board certified orthodontist Board certification is voluntary. It means a working orthodontist chose to have their finished cases examined by their peers. American Board of Orthodontics

03

How this practice runs

Three things we decided on purpose.

3.1

One orthodontist, every appointment.

There are no rotating associates here. Dr. Chang plans your treatment, makes the adjustments, and is the person you speak to when something needs changing. That continuity is the whole reason this practice stayed small.

Why families choose Chang Orthodontics

3.2

Appointments that fit around a school day.

Most of our patients are still in school, so after-school appointments are part of how the schedule is built rather than a favor you have to ask for. Wait times are kept short on purpose, because a checked-in patient sitting in a waiting room is a scheduling failure.

See what your first visit looks like

3.3

We live here too.

Dr. Chang and his family live in San Carlos, a few minutes from the office on El Camino Real. You will find the practice at community events around the Peninsula for the same reason: this is the neighborhood, not a catchment area.

Treatment by age

Orthodontics for children, teens, and adults.

Ages 7 to 11

Children

The first visit is a look, not a commitment. While the jaw is still growing there are things that are simple to correct now and difficult later, and plenty of children who need nothing yet.

  • Early orthodontics
  • Airway & expansion
  • Braces

Ages 11 to 18

Teens

Most of our patients are here. Braces or aligners, planned from a 3D scan rather than a putty impression, and scheduled around the school day so treatment does not cost a class every month.

  • Kids & teens
  • Braces
  • Invisalign

18 and older

Adults

Discreet aligners and ceramic braces, complex bite correction, and retreatment for teeth that shifted after earlier work. Nobody is too old for this.
