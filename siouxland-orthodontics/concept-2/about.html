Meet the Doctors

Two board-eligible orthodontists, one simple idea: you deserve to know your doctor, and your doctor should know you. Dr. Aaron Williams and Dr. Daniel Kaler care for Siouxland smiles side by side, at every one of our four offices.

Request a free consult

Dr. Aaron Williams: experienced, and genuinely glad you are here

Dr. Williams earned his DDS from the University of Iowa in 2010, then completed orthodontic specialty training and a master’s degree at the University of Colorado in 2013. He also holds an MBA. With more than ten years in practice, he has helped patients of every age feel confident about their smiles.

What patients notice first is not the diplomas. It is that he takes the time to explain what he sees, answers your questions in plain language, and never rushes a decision.

A Siouxland family, putting down roots

Dr. Williams and his wife, Laura, are raising five kids, so a busy family calendar is something he understands firsthand. He is active, artistic, and athletic, and he is proud to be planting roots and building relationships across the Siouxland community.

That family-first perspective shows up in the little things: appointments that respect your time, a team that treats you like a neighbor, and care that is built around real life.

Dr. Daniel Kaler: 30+ years of trusted care, still going

For more than three decades, Dr. Kaler has cared for Siouxland smiles, often straightening the teeth of two generations in the same family. He continues seeing patients alongside Dr. Williams, so the friendly, careful care your family has always trusted keeps right on going.

A board-eligible orthodontist and longtime member of this community, Dr. Kaler built the practice on plain answers, patient kindness, and results that speak for themselves. That is the legacy the whole team carries forward every day.
