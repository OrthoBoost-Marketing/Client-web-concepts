Sec. 01
The doubt

"Do those clear tray things actually work?"

A fair question, with a better answer than an ad.

You've seen the mail-order aligner ads and maybe the horror stories that followed them. So you're right to be skeptical: aligners are a tool, and like any tool, the result depends on who is holding it and whether it's the right tool for the job.

For the right case, worn as directed, clear aligners straighten teeth. Knowing which cases are right is specialist work: the kind a board-certified orthodontist does in person, with a scan of your actual teeth, not a photo uploaded to an app.

Questions

Before you commit to clear.

Invisalign questions, answered straight.

For the right case, worn as directed, clear aligners straighten teeth, and knowing which cases are right is the specialist's job. Dr. Romans's graduate research at Saint Louis University focused on Invisalign treatment outcomes, so his answer about whether aligners suit your teeth comes from study, not a sales script.

There's no honest flat answer. Aligner treatment is priced by what your teeth actually need. So we do it in person: your free consult ends with the exact price of your Invisalign plan, retainers included. PPO insurance and FSA/HSA funds can help, and in-house financing covers the balance.

You wear each set of aligners most of the day and night, taking them out only to eat, drink anything besides water, and brush. They're nearly invisible in conversation. Dr. Romans checks your progress personally at each visit and tells you exactly when to move to the next set.

A retainer. Teeth like to drift back toward where they started, so protecting the result matters as much as achieving it. Your retainer is part of the plan, and the price, from the very first consult. Our retainers page explains the options and how replacements work.
