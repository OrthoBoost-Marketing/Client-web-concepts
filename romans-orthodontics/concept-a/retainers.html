Sec. 01
The drift

"My teeth were straight once."

Teeth have a long memory. Retainers are how you win the argument.

Maybe the retainer got lost in a move. Maybe it snapped years ago and never got replaced, and lately you've noticed the front teeth quietly rearranging themselves. It's frustrating to watch something you paid for, in money and in months of brackets, slowly undo itself.

Teeth can keep shifting throughout life, which is why orthodontists treat retention as part of treatment rather than an epilogue. The good news: whether you need a replacement, a repair, or a plan to recover some lost ground, it starts with a simple scan.
