Sec. 01
The question

"Does my kid need braces yet?"

The honest answer starts with the timing.

Maybe the dentist mentioned crowding. Maybe you've noticed the front teeth coming in crooked, or a bite that doesn't quite line up. Most parents aren't sure whether to act now or wait, and worry equally about starting too soon and missing the window.

That's a fair worry, and it has a straightforward fix: an exam. The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first orthodontic check around age 7. Often the answer is "not yet. Let's watch and wait." When something does need doing, some kids benefit from a lighter first phase before full braces. Our early treatment page covers that path.
