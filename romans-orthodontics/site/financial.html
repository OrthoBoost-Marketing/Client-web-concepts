Sec. 02
Insurance

Plain-English version.

What orthodontic insurance actually does.

Dental insurance treats orthodontics differently from cleanings and fillings. Most plans that include orthodontic benefits contribute a portion of the cost up to a lifetime orthodontic maximum: a one-time cap that varies from plan to plan, separate from your annual dental maximum.

What a plan covers, for whom, and at what age also varies. Some plans cover orthodontics for children only; others include adults. Rather than guess, we verify your specific coverage at the free consult, so the price you take home already accounts for it.

Typically doesContribute a set portion of orthodontic treatment cost, up to the plan's lifetime orthodontic maximum.
Typically doesn'tCover the full cost of treatment, or renew the orthodontic benefit each year the way annual dental maximums do.
