Questions

Asked first, answered first.

Questions people ask before booking.

It depends on your case, so we won't print a number that might be wrong for you. Your free consult ends with an exact, personal price — treatment, retainers, everything. We accept major PPO plans and FSA/HSA funds, and offer in-house financing to spread the cost out.

No referral needed. You can book a free consult directly — that's exactly what it's for. Many families come to us straight from a dentist's suggestion, and just as many book on their own after noticing something about their child's bite or their own smile.

We accept major PPO dental plans and FSA/HSA funds, and we offer in-house financing for the remainder. Bring your insurance details to the free consult and we'll verify your orthodontic coverage on the spot, so the price you leave with is the real one.

Kids, teens, and adults. The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first orthodontic check around age 7 — often nothing needs doing yet, but catching timing early matters. And it's genuinely never too late: adult treatment is a large part of what Dr. Romans does.

Often the same week as your consult. Because there's one doctor and one office, scheduling is simpler here — no waiting for the next visiting provider. If you'd rather take the treatment plan home and think it over first, that's completely fine too.
