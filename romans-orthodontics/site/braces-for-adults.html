Sec. 01
The hesitation

"Aren't braces for teenagers?"

The thing most adults think, and why it's wrong.

Maybe you had braces once and your teeth drifted back. Maybe you never got them and always noticed. Either way, there's a quiet embarrassment adults carry into this decision — as if wanting a better smile past thirty needs a permission slip.

It doesn't. Healthy teeth can be moved at any age, and today's options are far more discreet than what you remember from middle school. What actually matters is the health of your teeth and gums — which is exactly what a free consult examines.

Sec. 02
Your options

Pick the hardware that fits your life.

Adult braces at Romans, without the guesswork.

A—01 / Metal The workhorse. Modern metal braces are smaller and more comfortable than the ones you remember, and Dr. Romans — board-certified by the American Board of Orthodontics — plans and adjusts them personally at every visit.
A—02 / Ceramic Tooth-colored brackets that blend in at speaking distance. A popular choice for adults who want fixed braces without announcing them in every meeting.
A—03 / Self-ligating A low-profile bracket design that holds the wire without elastic ties. Dr. Romans will tell you honestly whether it suits your case — not every option fits every mouth, and he'll say so.
A—04 / No brackets Rather skip braces altogether? Invisalign & clear aligners are nearly invisible — and Dr. Romans's graduate research focused on Invisalign treatment outcomes, so you'll get a properly informed comparison, not a coin flip.

Every option starts the same way: a digital scan instead of goopy impressions, and a plan built by the one doctor you'll see at every visit.
