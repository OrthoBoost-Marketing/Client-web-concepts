Meet your orthodontist

Dr. Nicholas Romans

Dr. Romans opened this practice in Anthem after more than 5,000 patients elsewhere, because he wanted an office where he knows every name on the schedule. Kids, teens and parents are all treated here: one orthodontist for the whole family, start to finish.

  • Board-certified orthodontist

  • More than 7 years dedicated to orthodontics, including advanced specialty training

Treatments

What we treat in Anthem

  • Braces for Kids

    →

    Metal and clear braces for growing smiles, checked at every stage. Short visits, each one explained to you and your child.

  • Braces for Adults

    →

    It is never too late, and plenty of our patients are grown-ups. Discreet options that fit around work and family life.

  • Invisalign® & Clear Aligners

    →

    Removable clear aligners planned with digital scans, no putty impressions. For teens and adults who would rather skip brackets.

  • Early Treatment

    →

    A first look around age seven, when small problems are still small. Often the visit ends with nothing to do but check back.

  • Retainers & Retention

    →

    The part that keeps the result. Fitted retainers, replacements, and a wearing plan people actually follow.

  • Airway & TMJ

    →

    Breathing, bite and jaw comfort assessed as part of your evaluation. Sometimes the jaw, not the teeth, is the thing to treat.
