Training built for hard cases, applied to ordinary ones.

Dr. Romans finished his MSD and orthodontic specialty training at Saint Louis University, then spent a craniofacial fellowship year with the Washington University team at St. Louis Children's Hospital, treating children with cleft lip and palate. In his own words: "More than 7 years dedicated to orthodontics, including advanced specialty training."

That depth is on hand for every bite he plans, including the straightforward ones. It is also why he reads your scans and x-rays himself rather than handing them to someone else.

The board certification

Dr. Nicholas R. Romans is certified by the American Board of Orthodontics. Certification is voluntary, it examines an orthodontist's own cases, and it is a matter of public record.

Where he trained

University of Kansas for his B.S. in Biology. A.T. Still University for his DMD, with a public health certificate earned alongside it. Saint Louis University for his MSD and orthodontic specialty training.

What the free consult includes

A tour, digital scans and imaging, an exam by Dr. Romans, a bite and jaw assessment, growth and airway evaluation, TMJ evaluation, treatment planning, and your written all-in price. No obligation.
