For parents

Bringing a nervous kid?

Most of what makes a first orthodontic visit stressful has nothing to do with orthodontics. Here is what you can tell them in the car.

  • Nobody puts putty in their mouth. We take a digital scan instead of the impression tray, so there is no gagging and no waiting with a mouthful of material.
  • One doctor, the whole time. Dr. Romans is the only orthodontist here. Your child meets him, not a rotating team, and he is the same person at every visit afterwards.
  • No stairs and no elevators. Suite D-120 is at ground level, straight in off the sidewalk, which matters with a stroller, a wheelchair or a sibling in tow.
  • Park right outside the door. Reserved patient parking sits directly outside the suite, so nobody arrives flustered from a lap of the parking lot.
  • English or Polish. Dr. Romans speaks both, so the visit can happen in whichever language your family is most comfortable in.
  • Nothing gets decided on the day. The consult is free, nothing is signed, and you leave with the written price to think about at home.
