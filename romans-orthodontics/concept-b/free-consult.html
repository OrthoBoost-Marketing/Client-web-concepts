What to expect

What happens at your free consult.

A look, and a scan

You get a tour, then a digital scan of your teeth instead of a tray of impression putty. Dr. Romans examines your bite, your jaw alignment and your airway himself. Nothing is rushed and nothing is handed off.

Your options, in plain words

He walks you through what he found, which treatments would actually work for your case, roughly how long each would take, and what he would recommend if it were his own family. Questions are the point of the visit.

Your exact price, in writing

You leave with one all-in written number covering treatment and retainers, with your insurance already verified and financing options laid out. Take it home and think it over. There is no pressure to decide.

Who you will actually see

The same orthodontist, start to finish.

Dr. Nicholas Romans is certified by the American Board of Orthodontics, meaning a specialist board examined his own clinical cases and not just his diploma. After specialty training he spent a fellowship year at St. Louis Children's Hospital caring for children with cleft lip and palate. He does your consult, and he is who you see at every appointment after it.
