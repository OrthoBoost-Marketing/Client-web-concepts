Meet your orthodontist

Dr. Nicholas Romans, DMD, MSD.

Ask him why he chose orthodontics and you get a story about people rather than teeth.

He earned a public health certificate alongside his dental degree, then spent a fellowship year caring for children with cleft lip and palate. On days off he still turns up at volunteer clinics. He built this practice on his own so that the person who plans your treatment is the person who finishes it.

Treatments

What we do in Anthem.

Braces and clear aligners for kids, teens and adults, all planned and finished by Dr. Romans.

Braces for Kids

Metal and ceramic braces sized for growing mouths, with a first visit built to be genuinely un-scary.

Braces for Adults

It is not too late and you are not too old. Discreet ceramic and self-ligating options for an adult schedule.

Invisalign & Clear Aligners

Removable, nearly invisible, planned by an orthodontist whose graduate research was on aligner outcomes.

Early Treatment

The first check belongs around age seven. Expanders when they help, and honest waiting when they do not.

Retainers & Retention

Permanent, Hawley and clear retainers, plus replacements when life happens. Treated elsewhere? Still welcome.

Airway & TMJ

Expansion for narrow arches, appliances for jaw joint pain, and complex bite correction.
