The person behind the practice.

Dr. Romans is a board-certified orthodontist and the founder, and only doctor, at Romans Orthodontics. Before Anthem, his path ran through A.T. Still University (DMD), Saint Louis University (MSD and orthodontic specialty training, with research on Invisalign outcomes), and a craniofacial fellowship with Washington University at St. Louis Children's Hospital, treating kids with complex conditions. He's cared for patients through Give Kids A Smile, Missions of Mercy, and Dentures for Veterans. Now he's here.
