Reason 01

One orthodontist, start to finish

In a lot of practices the doctor who plans your case is not the doctor who adjusts it, and the person who says it is finished has met you twice. Here there is one orthodontist, so the diagnosis, the wire changes, the debond and the retainer check are all the same judgment applied by the same person. It also means appointments are not rushed to clear a shared schedule.

“Dr. Romans has a way of explaining things clearly and honestly. You never feel rushed, and it’s obvious he wants people to make informed decisions about their care.”

Bijan Shemirani, patient review

Reasons 02 & 03

Board-certified, and trained past the residency

Board certification by the American Board of Orthodontics is voluntary. An orthodontist can practise a full career without ever submitting finished cases to a panel of examiners. Dr. Romans did. Behind it: a biology degree from the University of Kansas, a DMD and a Certificate in Public Health from A.T. Still University, an MSD from Saint Louis University with orthodontic specialty training and research into Invisalign outcomes, then a craniofacial cleft and palate fellowship through Washington University, based at St. Louis Children’s Hospital.

“As a fellow orthodontist, I had the privilege of working with Dr. Romans for over 2 years. Dr. Romans knows how to deliver a beautiful smile AND a healthy bite that will last a lifetime…”

Dr. Jacob Shelley, a fellow orthodontist. This is a peer review, not a patient’s.

Reason 05

Your insurance, checked while you sit there

Major PPO plans are verified during the consultation rather than mailed out as an estimate a week later. FSA and HSA funds apply. Whatever the plan does not cover can be financed in house, without a third-party lender in the middle. The consultation itself is free, and the figure you leave with is exact rather than a range.
