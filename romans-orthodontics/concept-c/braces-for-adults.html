Why you are here

Wondering if you are too old for braces.

Most adults who call us have been thinking about it for years. There is usually one tooth that has always bothered you, or a bite that has quietly got worse, and a nagging sense that the moment for fixing it passed somewhere around the eleventh grade.

Then comes the second thought, the real one: what it will look like in a meeting on Monday. You picture the mouthful of metal you saw on a classmate in 1998 and decide you would rather live with the tooth. That is a fair worry, and it is worth saying out loud before anyone talks to you about treatment.

Straight answers

Adult braces questions, answered.

Am I too old for braces?

No. Teeth respond to gentle pressure at any age. What changes with age is what the plan has to work around: existing crowns and fillings, gum health, and a bite that has settled into place over decades. Dr. Romans looks at all of it at your free consult before he recommends anything.

How much do adult braces cost in Anthem?

It depends on your case, and you get the exact number in writing before you commit to anything. Every consult here is free and ends with one all-in written price covering treatment and retainers, yours to keep. We do not publish figures on this page because a range that does not match your mouth is not useful to you. Major PPO dental plans are verified during the visit, FSA and HSA funds are accepted, and in-house financing is arranged directly with the practice. See insurance and financing.

Will my braces be obvious at work?

Less obvious than the braces you remember. Ceramic brackets are tooth-colored, and self-ligating brackets do without the elastic ties, so there is less hardware on each tooth. If you would rather nothing showed at all, ask about Invisalign and clear aligners, which come out for a presentation or a lunch.

How long does treatment take for an adult?

It depends on how far your teeth and your bite have to move, and you get that estimate in writing at the same consult as the price. Crowding alone is a different job from a bite problem sitting underneath the crowding. Either way you will know the expected length before you agree to start, not after.

Does dental insurance cover braces for adults?

Sometimes, and it is worth checking rather than assuming. Orthodontic benefits work against a lifetime orthodontic maximum, which is a different pot from your annual dental maximum, and coverage for adults varies from plan to plan. We accept major PPO dental plans and verify yours during the consult, so the written price you leave with already reflects what your plan does. FSA and HSA funds are accepted too.

Do I have to wear a retainer after my braces come off?

Yes, and it is not an upsell: teeth drift for the rest of your life. Retainers are included in the written price you get at the consult, so retention is not a surprise cost at the end of treatment. Permanent, Hawley and clear retainers are all options, and Dr. Romans will tell you which one suits your case. More on retainers and retention.
