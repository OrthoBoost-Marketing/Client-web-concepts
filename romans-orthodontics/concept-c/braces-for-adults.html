Questions

What adults ask before they start.

How much do braces for adults cost in Anthem?
It depends on how far your teeth have to move and how long that takes, so we do not print a single figure. The consultation is free, and you leave it with an exact number for your case, your insurance checked, and the monthly options written down. Nothing is estimated later by mail.
Am I too old for braces?
No. Teeth move at any age because the bone around them keeps remodeling through adult life. What changes with age is planning, not possibility: gum health, old dental work and missing teeth all shape the approach. Adults who were told as teenagers that their window had closed are a routine case here, not an exception.
Will braces be obvious at work?
Clear ceramic brackets sit against the tooth and read as much quieter than metal from across a table. Metal brackets are smaller than they used to be as well. If visibility is the deciding factor, ask about clear aligners at the consultation and compare the two plans side by side before you commit.
How long does adult treatment take?
That depends on the case. Simple crowding is a short course; a bite that needs correcting or a tooth that has to be brought down takes longer. You get an expected length at the consultation, after the scan, rather than a figure guessed before anyone has looked at your teeth. It is reviewed as treatment goes on.
Do you work with my dentist?
Yes. Adult cases often need more than one set of hands: a periodontist for gum work, a general dentist for a crown or an implant site. Dr. Romans coordinates the sequence with them so the appointments happen in the right order and you are not left carrying messages between offices.
Does my insurance cover adult orthodontics?
Some plans include adult orthodontic benefits and some cover dependents only, so it has to be checked rather than assumed. Major PPO plans are verified during your visit, not mailed as an estimate afterward. FSA and HSA funds apply, and any remaining balance is financed in house.
