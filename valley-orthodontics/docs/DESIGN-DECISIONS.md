# Homepage design decisions

## User direction, 9 September 2026

All three concepts must avoid generic AI-generated design conventions. Preserve the user's distinct reference directions: local practice for Concept 1, appointment-led for Concept 2, and editorial typography with the real doctor portrait for Concept 3.

- Use direct, descriptive headings and verified practice information. Avoid paired slogan fragments, smile metaphors, "next chapter" language and invented patient quotations.
- Use the actual practice logo and photography. Do not add repeated logo ornaments, invented credentials, decorative stars or oversized pseudo-statistics.
- Do not put every paragraph in a card. Group related content with spacing and simple rules. Use contained panels only when they make an actual task easier to understand.
- Avoid decorative numbered labels, Roman numerals, eyebrow labels on every section, corner decorations and arrows appended to every link.
- Keep appointments honest. There is no live scheduler. Concept 2 shows weekday office hours directly and uses phone booking; there are no day selection controls or implied available appointments.
- Preserve visible focus, native disclosure controls, mobile navigation, correct phone links, accurate office information and the approved client logo assets.
- Keep all concepts local and noindex until launch authorization and required integrations are supplied.

This direction supersedes earlier decorative treatments described in implementation notes.
