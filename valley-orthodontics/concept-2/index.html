Frequently asked questions

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Your consultation at Valley Orthodontics is free. Call (907) 376-1510 to arrange a visit.

Appointments are arranged by phone. Our team will help you find a time when you call.

Do you treat adults?

Yes. Dr. Haines provides orthodontic care for adults as well as kids and teens. Ask about braces and Invisalign at your consultation.

You can explore braces and Invisalign with Dr. Haines before choosing a path. Bring your questions and goals to your free consultation.
