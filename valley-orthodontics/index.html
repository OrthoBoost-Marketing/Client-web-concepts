Worth knowing before you look

  • Nothing here is live. All three are preview pages carrying a noindex tag, so they cannot appear in Google or compete with valleyorthoak.com.
  • There is no form on the homepage, by design. Booking is by phone until the appointment page and its delivery are wired up and tested. Every button says the same thing: schedule your free consultation.
  • All three now wear your own colours. Valley Gold, mist, slate and ink from your colour sheet, used three different ways. Headings are set in a condensed face that echoes the wordmark; the body face is warm and easy to read at 18px. If you have the original wordmark font, send it and we will match it.
  • Cost, hours and the phone number are in the first screen of every concept, and every page carries the same seven parent questions, three of your Google reviews word for word, and photographs of your board certificate and Invisalign Gold plaque as they hang on the wall.
  • The hero shows Dr. Haines alone or with the team for now. A photo of him with a patient goes in as soon as the photo releases come back.
  • Numbers now appear where your records confirm them. The 5.0 Google rating and review count, the $5,000 to $6,500 treatment range, payments from $125 a month, the $700 to $1,000 down payment and the office's 20+ years in the Valley all come from the details you gave us. Dr. Haines' own years in practice stay out until you confirm the figure (we hold both 10 and 13).
  • Office hours now read Tuesday to Thursday, 7:30 to 4:30 (to 4:00 in summer), with phones answered Monday to Friday, as you told us on 4 September. Send us the dates the summer hours start and end and we will add them.
  • Two more details from your own walls and feeds. Dr. Haines' Invisalign Gold Provider plaque (2025) is listed with his credentials, and the pages link the Instagram account you post to, @valleyorthodonticsalaska, with the older @valley_ortho_ak kept in the background data. Tell us if either should change.
  • All three now carry the same six reviews and the same seven questions. The reviews are yours from Google, quoted word for word. The questions are written so a person, or the AI answer at the top of a search, gets the answer in the first sentence.
  • Links to treatment and other pages point at your current website, since this round covers the homepage only.
