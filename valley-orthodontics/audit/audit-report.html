What this report is, and is not

It records what a script can verify: counts, paths, markup structure, and real measurements taken in a browser against the served tree. Tiers 1 to 4 block launch; tier 5 is fixed before handoff. When this audit conflicts with the design, the design changes.

It cannot tell a real patient photo from a stock one, a verbatim review from a paraphrase, or a figure that traces to the brief from one that was invented. Those are judgment checks, and a clear mechanical report is not a substitute for them.

Findings are grouped by rule, because one fix usually closes every page at once. Ticking a row records that the rule was addressed, not that a page was visited. Ticks are stored in this browser only, against this run's id, and are never carried into another run.

Still with a human

No script produces this list, and it is the most useful part of the report to a copy reviewer.

  • Every figure on the page, checked against CLIENT-BRIEF.md one at a time. Not by grep: a keyword-narrow sweep for "board-certified" once missed "certified orthodontic specialists".
  • Every review quote, verbatim against the client's Google profile, trimmed only with an ellipsis.
  • Every credential, certification, membership and award, against what the practice currently holds.
  • Real photography everywhere except the services grid. No stock humans, no AI-generated patients.
  • Every USP against the competitor paste test: if another office could run the heading verbatim, it is wrong.
  • Section cardinality, judged from the inventory rather than assumed. The engine scripts only the header social-icon and duplicate-tel checks, one H1 per page, duplicate ids and the footer legal links; every count rule is hand-judged.
